### 2. Croatian National Legal Layer (PSK)

1. **Act on Games of Chance (*Zakon o igrama na sreću*, NN 87/08, 35/14, 158/19, 114/22)**:
   - **Regulator**: Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Croatia (*Ministarstvo financija RH*).
   - **Registry Check Verification**: Real-time programmatic check against the National Register of Excluded Players (*Registar isključenih igrača*). Accounts flagged or voluntarily excluded are blocked from placing tickets, spins, or joining squads.
   - **RNG Standards**: Casino reels operate strictly on certified Random Number Generator (RNG) logic with transparent RTP (Return to Player) disclosure (>96%).
2. **Regulation on Measures for Socially Responsible Organisation of Games of Chance**:
   - Prominent, persistent display of the universal **18+ Age Restriction Gate** across every interface.
   - Direct helpline links to the Croatian Institute for Public Health (*HZJZ*) and national responsible gambling support hotlines.
3. **Croatian GDPR Implementation Act (*Zakon o provedbi Opće uredbe o zaštiti podataka*)**:
   - Supervised by the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (**AZOP**). All data processing boundaries adhere strictly to national data residency and security protocols.

### 3. Core Responsible Gaming & Safety Net Innovations

- 🛡️ **Student 50% Loss Refund Guarantee**: University students verified through campus credentials receive an automated, immediate 50% reimbursement into their wallet balance upon any settled loss across sports tickets, casino spins, or squad battles. This cuts downside volatility by half and prevents tilt-driven chasing behavior.
- ⏱️ **Reality Checks & Session Time-Outs**: Clear display of active session duration, total wagers, and net balance changes to maintain continuous financial self-awareness.
- 🛑 **Strict Self-Imposed Limits**: Instant user access to configurable daily, weekly, and monthly deposit and stake limits with a mandatory 24-hour cooling-off period for any limit increases.
- 🚫 **Zero Dark Patterns Policy**: Transparent odds calculations, no disguised losses as wins, no false near-miss animations, and zero hidden subscription or auto-debit fees.
- 🧪 **Anonymized Synthetic Data Only**: In compliance with FEG Hackathon ground rules, all player histories, ticket IDs, and transaction metrics are strictly synthetic and anonymized.
